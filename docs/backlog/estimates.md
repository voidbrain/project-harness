# Estimate calibration

Read before you close a step or re-estimate open steps. Part of the [backlog rules](../BACKLOG-RULES.md).

## Estimate calibration

**Estimate calibration.** When a step closes, record what it took next to its baseline and set `effort.reviewed` to the close date: `effort.actualAgentHours` (or `effort.actualDays` for a step still in days) with `effort.actualSource`. For a step in agent hours, an actual is the agent's elapsed time from starting the step to the merge of its last PR, reviews and CI waits included, the same quantity the estimate predicts: `measured` when it comes from the work-usage elapsed time of the jobs linked to the task, `stated` when it is the agent's or owner's own account. For a step still in days, `measured` is the active effort of the linked jobs in hours divided by `workItems.estimation.hoursPerDay` (default 6), never wall-clock time with waits. A stated figure is never shown as measured. When the time is not known (for example a fresh session that cannot tell when the step started), record no actual and write `No actual: <reason>` in the basis: never invent a figure. A cancelled or superseded step records no actual effort; a zero actual is not a sample. While a step is in progress (not while it is only planned), the actual may hold the effort spent so far; the steps table shows Effort (the size of the remaining agent time), Estimate (the latest whole-step estimate), Actual and Remaining side by side (a step without a recorded actual shows `0 h` or `0 days` with no source; a recorded zero shows its source, and the total row says how many steps recorded one); the baseline stays in the task file for calibration and is not shown (F-063 retires 6.2.0's `(planned …)` suffix), a step back in `planned` drops its actual, and only closed steps are calibration samples. `node tools/work-items.mjs calibrate` compares actual with baseline for closed steps, overall and by size, source, unit and kind (`bug` for B- steps, `feature` for F- steps; the same formula for both), with sample counts; it never rewrites an estimate. `check:freshness` asks for a re-estimation when, among steps closed since `workItems.estimation.since`, at least `minSamples` (default 5) have a median actual/baseline outside 1/`ratio`–`ratio` (default 1.5), overall or, when both kinds closed steps, within one kind on its own, or one step is `outlier` (default 3) times off either way. Then revise the remaining estimate (`agentHours` with `estimateAgentHours`, or `days` for a step still in days) of the open steps with a dated basis citing the calibration, keep every baseline, propose a material increase to the owner as [feature plans](feature-plans.md) says, and set `since` to the day of the re-estimation: only steps closed after it count, so the same samples do not trigger again. `workItems.estimation: false` turns the reminder off.
